Appendix G - Pathway Simulation

This appendix describes the simulation as implemented in _futureSim_refactored/.

This approach involves three key elements:

  • propositional community: generates proposals
  • evaluative community: negotiates differences and decides with mandate
  • pathways: show the visible consequences of these interactions over time

SUMMARY

Regions Modified By Proposals

  • habitat feature is a composite volumetric description of a tree, log, pole, or artificial structure.
  • under canopy is the immediate space under or around a feature.
  • surrounding region is the larger connected region around a feature. [in the manuscript, drop this one]
  • expanded space is the connected space between features made available by the connectivity simulation.

Proposals

  • Proposals can request:
    • modifications to existing habitat features that change their variant, such as Decay requesting a large feature continue to their senescing, snag and fallen lifecycle varient.
    • insertion of new habitat features such as sapling features under Recruit or artificial features under Deploy Structure.
    • modification to the expanded space that connects features, such as under Colonise.

Proposals are triggered by:

  • events (such as Decay)
  • latent changes (such as under Release Control)
  • respond to opportunities in environment (such as under Colonise responding to connectivity potential of the growth simulation)
  • or are enabled by previous proposals (such as Recruit and Deploy-Structure requesting insertion of new habitat features in areas unlocked by a previous Colonise proposal.

We model five proposals:

  • Individual tree features propose Decay when useful_life_expectancy reaches 0, requesting changes to the habitat feature, under canopy, and surrounding region that support progression through the lifecycle phases of senescing, snag, fallen, and decayed, or replacement.
  • Individual tree features propose Release Control at each pulse, requesting under canopy changes that support progression towards their moderated variant, and surrounding region changes that support progression towards their unmanaged variant.
  • Populations propose Colonise wherever the connectivity simulation makes expanded space available, requesting changes to ground, roof, and facade surfaces.
  • Populations propose Recruit at each pulse wherever Decay, Release Control, and Colonise have made under canopy, surrounding region, or expanded space available for new saplings.
  • Populations propose Deploy-Structure at compatible locations reached by Colonise, requesting changes that support artificial, hybrid, or fallen features.

Interventions

Communities can fully or partially support proposals through Interventions. Interventions allow proposals to proceed (or modify them) through changes made to:

  • habitat features, such as brace partially supporting a Decay proposal.
  • the area immediatly under or around a habitat feature, such as buffer fully supporting a Decay proposal by converting this area into a habitat island
  • the area connecting features, such as modifications ground, roof, and facade surfaces in response to Colonise proposals.

The interventions we model are (broken down by their proposal family):

Decay - brace-feature provides partial support by retaining tree features through senescing and snag, but not collapse. - buffer-feature provides full support by modifying under canopy and surrounding region to support progression through fallen and decayed.

Release Control - reduce-canopy-pruning provides partial support by modifying under canopy to support progression towards a moderated variant. - eliminate-canopy-pruning provides full support by modifying the surrounding region to support progression towards an unmanaged variant.

Colonise - roughen-envelope provides partial support by converting facades in expanded space into biofacades, and rooftops into brown roofs. - enrich-ground provides full support by converting ground in expanded space into habitat ground. - enrich-envelope provides full support by converting load-bearing roofs in expanded space into green-roofs.

Recruit - rewild-smaller-patch provides partial support by inserting saplings in under canopy zones. - rewild-larger-patch provides full support by inserting saplings in under canopy linked, surrounding region, or expanded space.

Deploy-Structure - translocate-log provides full support by inserting fallen, or fallen and decayed, features. - adapt-utility-pole provides full support by inserting an artificial canopy feature. - upgrade-feature provides full support by converting an introduced tree to its hybrid variant.

Overall Model

The simulation has: - the node layer stores habitat features and carries state forward from pulse to pulse. - the possibility-space layer stores the site as a voxel grid and evaluates current spatial opportunity. - a library of habitat features that define possible conditions of trees, logs and artificia; canopies.

Pathways emerges from repeated exchange between the two: node states are updated, projected into space, and used to recompute the conditions for later change.

Possibility-space layer

A pre-baked 3D voxel grid (possibility_space_ds) classifies where proposals can spread, connect, or be read spatially.The dataset can be used as a temporary spatial layer during a pulse (e.g. for computing recruitment zones) and is reset per pulse. It is also used at VTK generation time to broadcast node-level decisions onto voxels.

Its key fields are analysis_combined_resistance, analysis_combined_resistanceNOCANOPY, node_CanopyID, sim_Nodes, sim_Turns, and sim_averageResistance.

Evaluative Community

The evaluative community negotiates differences and decides with mandate. Interventions represent how the evaluative community responds to proposals. An intervention changes attributes of one voxel or a group of voxels in response to a proposal. The model applies interventions where voxel affordances match intervention requirements.

Whether an intervention proceeds depends on its effort threshold. The model compares resistance at voxels marked by a proposal with the threshold and allocates an intervention when the threshold exceeds resistance. Interventions use either feature or connectivity resistance depending on whether they target individual habitat features or connections. When multiple interventions fit, the model selects the highest effort option.

1. Proposal-Decay

Determines the lifecycle <size> variant for living trees once they progress beyond their large phase. Allows trees to enter the senescing -> snag -> fallen -> decayed pathway.

1.1 Proposal trigger

A tree proposes Decay when presenscent_duration_years <= 0.

The engine seeds presenscent_duration_years as:

useful_life_expectancy + triangular(-10, 0, 10)

Many trees use useful_life_expectancy from the input data. If that is missing, the engine falls back to:

  • small = 80 years
  • medium = 50 years
  • large = 10 years

1.2 Proposal assessment

The engine accepts Decay when:

CanopyResistance < minimal-tree-support-threshold

Otherwise it rejects the proposal.

1.3 Rejected outcome

The engine replaces the tree with a new precolonial sapling. It:

  • first defines replacement_growth_years
  • sets this as the number of years useful_life_expectancy has dropped below zero, capped at the pulse length
  • reseeds DBH from a 2 cm starting size, then grows it forward for between replacement_growth_years and the full pulse length
  • resets useful_life_expectancy to 120 - replacement_growth_years
  • resets presenscent_duration_years to: useful_life_expectancy + triangular(-10, 0, 10)

1.4 Supported outcome

Intervention Full/Partial Support What effort threshold Changes Effect
brace-feature partial moderate-tree-support-threshold < CanopyResistance < minimal-tree-support-threshold under canopy becomes exoskeleton retains the tree through senescing and snag, but does not allow full collapse in place
buffer-feature full maximum-tree-support-threshold < CanopyResistance <= moderate-tree-support-threshold under canopy becomes footprint-depaved opens the under canopy region and allows full decay in place
buffer-feature full CanopyResistance <= maximum-tree-support-threshold surrounding region becomes node-rewilded opens the surrounding region and allows full decay in place

1.5 State change

Once Decay is accepted, both brace-feature and buffer-feature allow the tree to move into senescing, then snag.

The senescing values are:

  • precolonial trees: triangular(10, 90, 200)
  • non-precolonial trees: @TODO link to royal-parade-elm-lifespan-appendix.md and briefly explain the elm useful-life framing triangular(10, 70, 110)
  • and for non-precolonial trees, 30% also get a sudden-death track: triangular(0, 25, 40)

After that:

  • snag duration: triangular(0, 40, 100)

Then the support split is:

  • Partial support
    • stops at snag
    • when snag duration ends, the engine replaces the tree through brace-collapse, using the same replacement path as a rejected Decay proposal
  • Full support
    • can continue to fallen
    • fallen to decayed: triangular(10, 40, 100)
    • decayed to gone: triangular(30, 40, 75)

2. Proposal-Release-Control

Determines the human management <control> modifying factor for living trees. Allows trees to progress towards their moderated <control = park-tree> or unmanaged <control = reserve-tree / improved-tree> variant.

2.1 Proposal trigger

A tree proposes Release Control in every pulse when its size is small, medium, or large.

2.2 Proposal assessment

The engine accepts Release Control when a tree’s CanopyResistance is below moderate-tree-support-threshold.

Otherwise it rejects the proposal.

2.3 Rejected outcome

The engine leaves the tree in its risk-averse variant <control = street-tree>.

It: - sets proposal-release-control_intervention to none - resets proposal-release-control_years to 0

2.4 Supported outcome

Intervention Full/Partial Support What effort threshold Changes Effect
reduce-canopy-pruning partial maximum-tree-support-threshold < CanopyResistance <= moderate-tree-support-threshold under canopy becomes footprint-depaved allows the tree to move towards its moderated variant
eliminate-canopy-pruning full CanopyResistance <= maximum-tree-support-threshold surrounding region becomes node-rewilded allows the tree to move towards its unmanaged variant

2.5 State change Once Release Control is accepted, every pulse, the engine adds step_years (the pulse length) to a tree features’ proposal-release-control_years.

  • when proposal-release-control_years = 20, under reduce-canopy-pruning or eliminate-canopy-pruning, the tree becomes its moderated variant <control = park-tree>
  • when proposal-release-control_years = 40, under eliminate-canopy-pruning only, the tree becomes its unmanaged variant <control = reserve-tree / improved-tree>

Trees in senescing, snag, fallen, or decayed are always under the unmanaged variant <control = improved-tree>. #### 3. Proposal-Colonise

Determines where the model classifies expanded space as available for ground and envelope colonisation.

3.1 Proposal trigger and assessment

The engine proposes and accepts Colonise where expanded space has been reached by the connectivity model within the current sim_TurnsThreshold.

3.2 Rejected outcome

The engine does nothing.

3.3 Supported outcome

Intervention Full/Partial Support What effort threshold Changes Effect
larger-patches-rewild full sim_Turns <= sim_TurnsThreshold and urban element is ground sets scenario_bioEnvelope to otherGround or rewilded changes persona indicators and triggers Recruit and Deploy proposals

3.4 State change

Colonise changes attributes of voxels and triggers Recruit, Release Control, and Deploy proposals. #### 4. Proposal-Recruit Determines where the model inserts new sapling habitat features.

4.1 Proposal trigger

We simulate Recruit as a population-level event. The engine proposes Recruit at every pulse where there is available space in candidate zones, but scales establishment to a 30 year recruitment interval following other ecological tree models.13

4.2 Proposal assessment

The engine assesses Recruit through two interlinked phases:

  1. under-node treatments proposed by Decay and Release Control, together with expanded space unlocked by Colonise, determine the size and type of recruitment zones available.
  2. quota determines how many saplings can be inserted into each recruitment zone.

Recruitment quota this pulse = area of recruitment zone × planting density × recruitment interval × support multiplier.

We define planting density as 50 trees/ha every 30 years, following established woodland and urban tree recruitment densities.14

Support multiplier settings:

  • positive: rewild-larger-patch = 1.6, rewild-smaller-patch = 1.2
  • trending: both 1.0

The engine then subtracts existing occupancy and tries to place saplings into the remaining slots.

Accepted saplings must be placed at least 1.5 m apart.

4.3 Rejected outcome

The engine does nothing.

4.4 Supported outcome

Intervention Full/Partial Support What effort threshold Changes Effect
rewild-smaller-patch partial the under canopy area of a feature has been set to footprint-depaved sets recruit_multiplier_partial and annual_tree_death_urban inserts a quota of saplings in under canopy zones
rewild-larger-patch full more than 50% of under canopy area overlaps ground voxels already activated by Colonise
or the surrounding region of a feature has been set to node-rewilded
or ground voxels have already been activated by Colonise		 sets recruit_multiplier_full and annual_tree_death_nature-reserves inserts a quota of saplings in under canopy linked, surrounding region, or expanded space

4.5 State change

Each accepted placement appends a new precolonial sapling.

It:

  • reseeds DBH from a 2 cm starting size, then grows it forward within the same pulse
  • sets useful_life_expectancy to 120
  • sets presenscent_duration_years to 120 + triangular(-10, 0, 10)
  • marks the new row as proposal-recruit_accepted
  • assigns low-control management <control = reserve-tree, control_reached = low-control>

5. Proposal-Deploy-Structure

The engine proposes Deploy-Structure where a location has been reached by Colonise within the current sim_TurnsThreshold, and a specific feature is present at that location, or there is potential for one.

5.1 Proposal trigger and assessment

The engine assesses Deploy-Structure through four paths:

  • translocated fallen trees on rooftops
    • The model first calculates roof area, then how much additional log weight that roof can support per square metre.
    • green roof = 50 kg/m²; brown roof = 100 kg/m²
    • It multiplies that value by roof area to get the total log load the roof can carry.
    • It then assigns logs from the log library.
    • It tries large, then medium, then small.
    • It checks whether each log’s Biomass_kg fits within the remaining roof load.
    • If it fits, it accepts it and places it on a randomly chosen eligible roof voxel.
    • It continues until the remaining roof load is exhausted, or no more logs of that size can fit.
  • fallen and decayed trees on colonised ground
    • The model identifies colonised ground and calculates how much fallen and decayed deadwood can be inserted there in the current pulse.
    • It then adds new fallen and decayed features until that pulse budget is filled.
    • @TODO link to baseline note reference on deadwood amounts.
  • adapted utility poles
    • The model identifies existing utility poles at locations reached by Colonise as candidates for artificial canopies.
  • upgrade-feature
    • The model identifies size = medium and size = large, precolonial = false tree features reached by Colonise as candidates for conversion from origin = introduced to origin = hybrid.

5.2 Rejected outcome

The engine does nothing.

5.3 Supported outcome

Intervention Full/Partial Support Supported outcome
translocate-log full inserts a fallen feature on roofs, or inserts fallen and decayed features on colonised ground
adapt-utility-pole full inserts an artificial canopy feature
upgrade-feature full converts an existing introduced tree to its hybrid variant

5.4 State change

Once Deploy-Structure is accepted, the model either enables an existing structure or inserts a new deadwood feature.

Accepted logs and poles remain as enabled structures.

Accepted ground deadwood enters the model as new fallen or decayed habitat features. ## References
